How I work

I’m usually at my best somewhere between strategy and implementation.

I can work with a team to figure out what we should build, work through the UX, help shape the technical approach, and stay involved long enough to see whether it actually worked.

Three things tend to matter most:

  • Clarity. Turning a messy problem into something a team can understand and act on.
  • Good decisions. Getting product, design, engineering and the business working from the same understanding of the problem.
  • Learning. Shipping, measuring, talking to customers and changing course when the evidence says we should.

I care about metrics, but I don’t believe everything important can be reduced to a number.

How I can help

I’m interested in difficult problems where product, people, design and technology all have a part to play.

Sometimes that means figuring out what to build. Sometimes it means helping a team work better together. Sometimes it means getting close to the product and building something myself.

I’m comfortable moving between those things, and I like working with people who care about solving the actual problem rather than protecting a particular role or process. That can mean joining a team in a leadership role, or working alongside one on a specific problem.
